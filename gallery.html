KMTNet Synoptic Survey of Southern Sky

NGC 4696 and the Centaurus cluster
The heart of the Centaurus galaxy cluster (Abell 3526). It is dominated by NGC 4696, a giant elliptical and one of the most luminous galaxies in the nearby Universe. Dozens of cluster members, from ellipticals and lenticulars to background spirals, are scattered across the field.
1.31° × 0.71°, BVI composite
NGC 2442, the Meathook Galaxy
A strongly lopsided barred spiral in Volans, about 15 Mpc away. One arm is long and diffuse, the other compact, a sign of a past tidal interaction.
21.1′ × 11.5′, BVI composite
NGC 5291 and its tidal dwarfs
A collisional ring system on the outskirts of Abell 3574. New tidal dwarf galaxies are forming in the debris ring, left by a high-speed collision about 360 Myr ago.
18.4′ × 10.1′, BVI composite
NGC 5292
A face-on disc galaxy in Centaurus. The stacked images resolve its spiral arms and central bar.
9.42′ × 5.15′, BVI composite
