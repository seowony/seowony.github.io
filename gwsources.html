Sources found in GW follow-up

When GECKO follows up a gravitational-wave alert with KMTNet, the image subtraction picks up many real sources that have nothing to do with the merger: variable stars, supernovae and other transients that were already known. They are a useful check that the search works. Each card shows the new, reference and difference images and the light curve measured in the follow-up images.
