Gravitational-wave follow-up

The GECKO team searches for the optical light of gravitational-wave events found by LIGO, Virgo and KAGRA. This page shows the alerts followed up with the 7-Dimensional Telescope (7DT) and KMTNet, whose pointings are reported to the Gravitational-wave Treasure Map. Each sky map shows the probability region of the event and the fields observed. Drag to turn the sky and scroll or pinch to zoom.
