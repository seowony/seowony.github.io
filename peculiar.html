Southern peculiar galaxies in KS4

This gallery shows peculiar and interacting galaxies, nearby spiral galaxies from the PHANGS-MUSE and PHANGS-JWST samples and a few other well-known objects, such as globular clusters and planetary nebulae, that lie south of declination −28° and fall inside the footprint of KS4 Data Release 1. The order changes each time the page is opened. Click an image to see it larger. The name under each image opens the KS4 BVI colour HiPS in Aladin Lite so you can pan and zoom around it. The white bar in the lower-right corner of each image is one arcminute, or five arcminutes on fields of 25 arcminutes or more.

13 of the 43 objects in the list are inside the KS4 DR1 footprint.

Images: KMTNet Synoptic Survey of Southern Sky (KS4) DR1, BVI colour HiPS (Chang et al. 2026, JKAS). Positions from SIMBAD and OpenNGC (CC BY-SA 4.0).
